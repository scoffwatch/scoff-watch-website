The story behind the mark

Heritage, reimagined for your own path.

SCOFF WATCH is an independent brand concept inspired by Norse heritage, resilience and individuality. The goal is to bring those influences into a modern everyday watch: strong lines, a dark dial, silver details and a symbol designed to become instantly recognisable.

The four-diamond emblem represents the identity we’re building — distinctive, considered and made to stand apart. Our first design is being developed with manufacturing partners, with quality, legibility and value at the centre of every decision.

We’re sharing the journey early and inviting interested customers to follow the first release as the design develops.
